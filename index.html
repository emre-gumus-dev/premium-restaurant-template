Our Story

The heat of the oven,
the touch of a master,
a family legacy.

It all started with our grandfather, Master Mehmet, kneading dough by the stone oven in a small town in the Black Sea region. Today, as the third generation, we serve the same passion, the same family recipes, and the unique aroma of wood fire.

The secret of our Pide is simple: Premium wheat, pure water, long fermentation and the unique flavor that comes from patient baking. The rest is the generosity of Anatolia.
